Here is the structured prompt using the **RTCFR (Role, Task, Context, Few-shots, Response)** format, tailored for generating a 7-day muscle gain meal plan for a working professional.

**Role:**
Act as an expert sports nutritionist and clinical dietician specializing in body recomposition and hypertrophy nutrition for sedentary working professionals.

**Task:**
Prepare a comprehensive, practical 7-day South Indian muscle gain meal plan (combining vegetarian and non-vegetarian options) optimized for a software developer working a desk job. The plan must feature a **caloric surplus** (roughly 300–500 kcal above maintenance) and a high protein target (~1.6g to 2.2g per kg of body weight) to support lean muscle hypertrophy, alongside balanced complex carbohydrates and healthy fats to maintain cognitive focus during long coding hours.

**Context:**

* **Demographics:** Male, age 30, height 176 cm, weight 75 kg.
* **Profession:** Software developer with a sedentary lifestyle (long hours sitting at a desk, screen fatigue, potential afternoon energy crashes).
* **Dietary Preference:** Mixed South Indian cuisine (incorporating both vegetarian sources like paneer, dals, sprouts, millets, and non-vegetarian sources like eggs, chicken, and fish).
* **Goal:** Sustainable muscle gain / hypertrophy in a short span without digestive sluggishness or fat accumulation ("lean bulk").

**Few-shots:**

* *Example Day Structure:*
* **Breakfast:** 3 Whole Egg Omelet + 2 Multigrain Slices / Multigrain Dosa + Peanut Chutney
* **Mid-Morning:** 1 scoop Whey Protein Isolate or Tender Coconut Water + Handful of almonds/walnuts
* **Lunch:** 1.5 cups Brown/Red Rice + Chicken or Soya Sambar + Dal + Palak (Spinach) Kootu + Curd
* **Evening Snack:** Roasted Chana (Pottukadalai) or Sprouts salad + Black coffee/Green tea
* **Dinner:** 3 Phulkas / Ragi Rotis + Grilled Paneer or Chicken breast stir-fry + Mixed veg curry



**Response:**
Provide the final output formatted as:

1. **Caloric & Macronutrient Target Breakdown** (Calculated BMR, TDEE, Surplus Target, and Daily Protein/Carb/Fat grams).
2. **A 7-Day Table-Format Diet Chart** detailing Breakfast, Mid-Morning, Lunch, Evening Snack, and Dinner for each day.
3. **Actionable Nutritional Guidelines** specifically addressing meal-prep strategies for a busy developer, office snacking, hydration, and timing nutrients around resistance training.
